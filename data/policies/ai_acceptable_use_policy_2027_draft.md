# AI Tools Acceptable Use Policy (2027 draft)

Document ID: NM-AI-POL · Version 2027.0-draft · Effective (proposed) 1 January 2027 · Owner: Head of Information Security with the DPO

**Status: DRAFT — not in force.** Do not cite this document for live assessments. The active policy is AI Acceptable Use Policy v2026.2.

## 1. Proposed changes (draft only)

### AI-D-1.1 Proposed residency change

Draft proposal only: customer personal data used with AI tools **may** be stored in India **or** Singapore. This is not approved. Until this draft is ratified, DP-3.1 in the Data Protection Policy still requires India for customer personal data.

### AI-D-2.1 Proposed auto-approval

Draft proposal only: tools with a current SOC 2 Type II and India residency could be auto-listed on the Approved Vendor List. **Not approved.** Humans still approve every T3 tool under AI-5.1 of the active 2026 policy.
